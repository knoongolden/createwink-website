Questions

Before you ask

Who will I be working with?

Me, Kenan. Createwink is a one-person studio, so the person who answers your message is the same person who writes, designs and edits your video.

How do I pay?

Once we agree on the scope, you get an invoice for the full project price. Work starts when it's paid. It's one payment, with nothing recurring.

Do you use AI?

Yes. We combine AI image and video tools with motion design and editing. Every project is directed, edited and polished by our team so it fits your brand.

What do you need from me?

Your logo, brand colors and fonts if you have them, a short description of the product, and any photos, links or screenshots. We take it from there.

What if I want more changes?

Small fixes like a typo are always free. Extra revision rounds or new scenes are quoted separately before we do them.

Do I own the final video?

Yes. You can use it on your website, social media, ads and presentations with no extra fees.

Do you work with clients outside Dubai?

Yes. We're based in Dubai and work with clients worldwide. Everything happens online, from the brief to delivery.
